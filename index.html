Privacy Policy

iyadasla iptv · Last updated: November 5, 2026

iyadasla iptv (“the app”) is a media player for Mac (macOS) and Android TV. It lets you watch live streams from an IPTV service that you already subscribe to. The app does not include or provide any channels, playlists or content.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal information on our own servers. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third‑party tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device

To save you from retyping it, the app stores the server address, username and password that you enter on the sign‑in screen. This data is kept only on your device, in the app’s private storage, and is never sent to the developer. On Mac it is protected with the encryption provided by macOS when that is available. The app also remembers the last channel you watched, so it can reopen it next time; this is stored only on your device too. You can remove this data at any time by choosing “Disconnect”, deleting the app’s data, or uninstalling the app.

Information sent to your IPTV provider

When you sign in and watch, the app sends your server address, username and password, and requests for channel lists and streams, directly to the IPTV service you entered. This is how the service authenticates you and delivers video. That provider is independent from us and handles your data under its own terms and privacy policy. We have no access to this traffic.

Permissions

  • Internet access (outgoing network connections) – required to connect to your IPTV service and play streams.
  • Mac only: the app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and can only access the files you explicitly select.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Content

The app is a player only. We do not host, supply or endorse any streams. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to access the content you play.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Data security

Stored sign‑in details remain inside the app’s private storage on your device (the macOS App Sandbox on Mac, or Android’s app‑private storage). Some IPTV services use unencrypted (HTTP) connections; if your provider does, traffic between the app and that provider is not encrypted. Choose an HTTPS address if your provider offers one.

On Android, if you have device backup turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your Google backup. That is handled by your device and Google under your Google account settings; the developer does not receive it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
